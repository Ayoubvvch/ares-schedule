# Ares Schedule 🖤 (2BAC SP-4)

> Ultra-lightweight, blazing fast Android schedule application for Moroccan 2BAC Sciences Physiques (2026/2027).

---

## ✨ Features
* ⚡ **Instant Cold Startup:** Opens in less than 100ms right from your home screen.
* 🖤 **True OLED Pitch Black (`#000000`):** Designed for AMOLED screens with rich neon subject accents.
* 🔴 **Live Class Tracker:** Real-time countdown for ongoing sessions and "Up Next" preview.
* 📅 **Instant Day Switcher:** Seamlessly browse between study days (Mon–Sat) or view the full week at a glance.
* 📴 **100% Offline & Private:** Zero network requests, zero telemetry, zero background battery consumption.
